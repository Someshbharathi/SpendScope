## FAQ

### 1. Is my audit data public?

Only if **you** share it. By default stuff sits in your session; when you save, you get a **URL** you can pass around. Treat that like anything you’d paste in a channel—anyone with the link can open that snapshot. Don’t put numbers on there you wouldn’t email externally.

### 2. How accurate are the benchmarks?

We’re comparing what you typed to **published list-style pricing** for the plans and seats you picked. We’re **not** reading your invoice, your enterprise discount, or your API overage curve. So: useful for a conversation, not a replacement for accounting.

### 3. Do I need to connect billing APIs?

Nope. That’s why the thing is fast—and why we can’t see your real usage spikes. Tradeoff’s intentional for the MVP.

### 4. Why are some savings numbers low—or zero?

Sometimes you’re already near the benchmark we model, or the savings would be so small that showing them would be **noise** in a real renewal. I’d rather show nothing dramatic than fake a win.

### 5. How often is pricing updated?

By hand, when someone actually updates the table—tied to coursework or a release, not a live scraper hitting vendor sites every night. Before you renew, **check the vendor’s own page**; they’ll move before our repo does.
